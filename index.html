01 What It Is

The everyday things you use — your phone plan, your supermarket aisle, your salary slip, your medicine strip — were designed by someone, for reasons that were never disclosed to you.

With care.
With intent.
Without explanation.

Not an events company. Not a community platform. An institution — one that treats curiosity as something worth compounding.

02 The Operating Model
01

Branches

Brand partners who commit a venue, a subject, and someone who knows it from the inside. A café. A clinic. A brewery. A bank.

Each branch operates on a published schedule. A branch opening is a public event. The arrangement is ongoing.

02

Account Holders

Members who open an account, receive a passbook, and attend sessions across branches.

Attendance is the deposit. What they leave with is the return.

03

Banking Products

  • Savings Account A single ninety-minute session
  • Fixed Deposit A multi-week deep dive
  • Current Account A recurring drop-in

The language is not metaphor.
It is the structure.

10 For Branches

Branches are places where the Bank temporarily operates — partner institutions that open a door into how they work, once a month.

Become a branch.

A branch commits a venue, a subject, and a recurring schedule. In return: a room of people who came to learn, not to be sold to.

The name is on the door. The expertise is in the room. Branch openings are public. The relationship does not end after one session.
