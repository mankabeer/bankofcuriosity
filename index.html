What It Is

The everyday things you use — your phone plan, your supermarket aisle, your salary slip, your medicine strip — were designed by someone. Designed carefully, often brilliantly, and almost never explained to you.

The Bank exists to close that gap. Brands host sessions at their venues. Curious citizens show up. And in ninety minutes, something you interact with daily becomes something you understand.

It is not an events company. It is not a community platform. It is an institution — one that treats curiosity as a civic resource worth investing in.

How It Works

The operating model

01

Branches

Brand partners who provide a venue, a subject, and a point of view. A bookstore, a café, a brewery, a telecom showroom, a pharmacy.

Each branch hosts sessions during announced operating hours. A branch opening is a public event — a launch moment for the partnership.

02

Account Holders

Members. They open an account, receive a passbook, and attend sessions across branches.

Showing up is the minimum deposit. What they take home — a skill, a question, a contact — is the withdrawal.

03

Banking Products

Session formats co-designed by branches and the Bank.

  • Savings Account — A single ninety-minute session
  • Fixed Deposit — A multi-week deep dive
  • Current Account — A recurring drop-in

For Branches

Become a branch

A brand becomes a branch when it commits to hosting sessions on a recurring basis.

In return, it receives a curated, engaged audience that arrives not to be sold to, but to be surprised. The branch is a host, not a sponsor. Its name is on the door and its expertise is in the room.

A café branch might host a session on how coffee is graded before it reaches the menu. A pharmacy branch might host a session on how medicine strips are designed. The brand's own knowledge becomes the session — and the audience sees the brand differently afterward.

Branch openings are announced publicly. A small plaque marks the venue. The relationship is ongoing, not transactional.

For Account Holders

Open an account

There is no fee, no minimum balance, no obligation.

What you get is access: a calendar of sessions across branches, a passbook that records your deposits, and periodic statements summarising where you've been and what's coming.

What compounds is not points or perks. It is the thing curiosity actually produces when sustained: depth. Relationships with people who show up. Familiarity with how the designed world works. An instinct for asking better questions.
